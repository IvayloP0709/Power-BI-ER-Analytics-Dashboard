Information regarding the additionally added DAX measures needed for the visualization and dynamics of the showcased dashboard:
% Female Visits -	Percentage of total visits made by female patients.
% Male Visits -	Percentage of total visits made by male patients.
% of Non-Rating  Patients	Percentage of patients who did not provide a satisfaction rating.
% of Non-Referred - Patients	Percentage of patients who were not referred to another department.
% of Referred Patients -	Percentage of patients who were referred to another department.
% Unknown Visits -	Percentage of visits where the patient's gender or other classification is unknown.
Administrator % -	Percentage of visits or patients handled by administrative staff.
CF Min/Max Point (Month) -	The minimum or maximum data point for a specific metric within a given month.
CF Min/Max Point (Year) -	The minimum or maximum data point for a specific metric within a given year.
Heatmap Caption	A - dynamic label used for the interpratation of the heatmap at the bottom right corner, both for the mean satisfaction score and mean wait time 
Mean Satisfaction Score -	Average patient satisfaction score based on survey data.
Mean Wait Time -	Average time patients waited before being attended to.
Non-Administrator % -	Percentage of visits or patients handled by non-administrative staff (e.g., clinical).
Total Patients - Total count of patients in the dataset.
Values Min/Max Point (Month) -	minimum/max point used for the "Total Patient Visits per Year" visualization in order to color-code the lowest/highest point 
Values Min/Max Point (Year) -	minimum/maximum point used for the "Total Patient Visits per Month" visualization in order to color-code the lowest/highest point 

Furthermore, a slide made in powerpoint is presented, which is the background used for the design of the dashboard
